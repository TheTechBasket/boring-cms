# Ops follow-ups from the 2026-10-06 traffic and log review

**Source.** Cloudflare analytics for cms.thetechbasket.com (30 days to 2026-10-06, about 69.7k requests) and the server logs on hetzner-ashphp (nginx site error log, pm2 logs). Prod runs v0.24.1.

**Findings**
- About 57% of requests are scanner probes (`/.env*`, `/@fs/`, `/actuator`, `.git`, `wp-` paths). nginx already denies them ("access forbidden by rule", 15,096 lines in the error log), but each one still reaches the origin.
- MCP: about 16k requests, mostly POST calls from Amit's publish scripts. No errors.
- `articles` API: 7,743 GETs, all 200, zero 304. All from the one-off thetechbasket import scripts.
- 5xx: a burst on 2026-09-09 (launch day) and two 504s on rewrite-refs on 2026-09-11 (fixed in v0.17.1). Nothing since.
- Admin articles collection page: 930 ms average at the origin. Cause: 2 full-table JSON scans per field for the field-usage panel. Fixed in v0.25.0 (one scan for all fields).
- nginx error log: 51 "no live upstreams" and 18 "connection refused" (deploy restarts), 2 upstream timeouts, 10 `[::1]` lines (`proxy_pass http://localhost:3005`).
- pm2: online, 32 restarts (all deploys), error log empty. The node process RSS is 1.2 GB after 10 days of uptime.

Now within about 7% of v0.24.1 (the remainder is the `total` COUNT on uncached list reads).
- [x] Fix RSS growth in v0.25.0: response cache capped at 32 MB of bodies per db. After deploy, read the node RSS one day later; it must stay well under 1.2 GB.

With the real articles data, 500 cached pages cost +535 MB RSS at limit=50 and +919 MB at limit=100. The import scripts paged through many offsets under one ETag. On prod, 1,263 anonymous 256K-1M mappings (V8 heap pages) hold 1.16 GB. Fix: cap the cache by total body bytes per db (32 MB) in addition to the 500-entry count.

**WAF rule (draft, not applied)**

Option A, allowlist (recommended). The server has only these top-level paths: `/admin`, `/api`, `/mcp`, `/media`, `/public`, `/login`, `/logout`, `/setup`, `/account`, `/auth`, `/reset-password`, `/webauthn`, `/robots.txt`, `/`. Block every other path:

```
(http.host eq "cms.thetechbasket.com" and not http.request.uri.path in {"/" "/robots.txt" "/login" "/logout" "/setup"}
 and not starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/admin")
 and not starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/api/")
 and not starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/mcp/")
 and not starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/media/")
 and not starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/public/")
 and not starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/account")
 and not starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/auth/")
 and not starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/reset-password")
 and not starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/webauthn/"))
```

Action: Block. Risk: a new top-level route must be added to the rule, or it is blocked at the edge. `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource` (about 630 probes from MCP clients) is blocked too, which is correct: the server has no OAuth.

Option B, denylist. Smaller risk of blocking real traffic, but scanners change paths:

```
(http.host eq "cms.thetechbasket.com" and (starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/.env") or http.request.uri.path contains "/.git"
 or starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/@fs") or starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/actuator")
 or starts_with(http.request.uri.path, "/wp-") or ends_with(http.request.uri.path, ".php")))
```

- After you apply the rule, origin 403 and 404 counts in Cloudflare analytics must decrease. The nginx "access forbidden by rule" lines must stop.
